Create a high-resolution concept contact sheet, 2 columns x 2 rows, four equally sized landscape photographic panels, thin off-white gutters, tiny clean numeric labels 01 02 03 04 at top-left of each panel only. Overall landscape sheet. These are four DIFFERENT parallel worlds viewed through fixed observation cameras, plausible documentary photography of impossible places, not illustrations or CGI. Reference the intimate observational framing and photographic light of the WORLDS INSIDE the central screens of the supplied reference images ONLY. Do not reproduce any Console, screen borders, interface, warehouse exterior or existing characters. Each scene needs a legible inhabitant, everyday activity, and a subtle visible mystery. Keep central subjects suitable for a future circular crop. No heroes posing, no giant epic landscapes, no holograms, no glowing magical swirls, no text other than panel numbers.
01 THE LATE REFLECTION: a weathered human tidal caretaker in a small stone coastal washhouse, three-quarter fixed view. The real caretaker has set down a brass cup and is looking toward us while his reflection in the still indoor tidal pool is still lifting the cup to his mouth. Physically credible reflections except this deliberate temporal mismatch. Sea daylight, wet limestone, natural skin.
02 THE COLORLESS GARDEN: a gentle nonhuman gardener with pale amphibian skin and small eyes, work apron, examining healthy and unhealthy plants in a modest greenhouse. Green and red leaves look very different to us but the gardener has organized them by leaf shape, placing sick red-leaf plants beside healthy green plants. Tangible practical creature effects, no cartoon, diffused daylight. Plants and gardener fill frame.
03 THE MACHINE THAT STILL SETS THE TABLE: a utilitarian aging household robot, not humanoid armor, patiently laying six mismatched ceramic plates at an empty dining table in a sunlit abandoned apartment overgrown by small plants. It pauses with the sixth plate and looks toward an empty chair near the camera. Ordinary human traces, faded family photo turned away, poignant ambiguity, documentary realism.
04 THE HOUSE THAT ANSWERS: interior of a living organic house with believable woody ribs, woven wall membranes and breathing-looking fabric textures, an adult human resident sitting quietly at a wooden table, puzzled by a wall whose folds reproduce the outline of their hand. The house itself may be intelligent. Warm late afternoon light, tactile architecture, no horror, no faces in walls. Camera fixed in a doorway.
Make each panel feel like a place one could revisit for months. Clear exposure, restrained color, realistic optical perspective and surface detail.
