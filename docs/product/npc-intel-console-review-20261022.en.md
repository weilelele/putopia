## Context now required for every run

The repository instructions in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and the [NPC Intel runbook](npc-intel-runbook.zh.md) require the current full text of the Collective overview, Console narrative overview, Console operation specification, all NPC personality sections and the runbook before selecting participants or drafting text or media. Console operation rules take precedence over historical GDD descriptions. Each run records file hashes; missing or conflicting context leaves the affected action as a draft or skip.

The operation specification defines capabilities; it does not prove that a particular person has used them, or that a specific firmware or website implementation has passed acceptance. The current allocation lookup returned no rows for these four identities. That is not proof that they never used a Console, so the revisions avoid assigning an unverified first-ever experience.

## Review and proposed wording

### Luke B.

**Recommendation: Keep.** A provenance question fits both Luke's archival background and the documented handoff history of individual Consoles. It does not invent storage, recording or transfer features.

Original published text:

> Will a little of each Console's history go with it when it changes hands? Even a note about where it came from would be worth keeping.
> 
> [Fictional NPC]

Retain the published wording.

**Image decision:** The question is already concrete. A fabricated old log or provenance document would add unsupported history.

### June K.

**Recommendation: Refine.** No hardware contradiction, but the broad question is weakly grounded in the known operation. A neutral request about recording Frequency, Band, time and changes preserves her curiosity without claiming a first-ever experience.

Original published text:

> Okay, I'm curious about the really ordinary bit. What does a first observation actually look like? Do you note the time and what was around you, or just what caught your eye? I'd probably have ten things written down before I worked out which ones mattered.
> 
> [Fictional NPC]

Approved replacement (now published):

> I'd start with the Frequency and Band, then the time and anything that changed in the view. What else do you find worth writing down? I can see myself filling a whole page before working out which bits actually mattered.
> 
> Picture: an idea for a notebook layout, not an observation log. AI-generated illustration.
> 
> [Fictional NPC]

**Image decision:** A blank notebook layout makes June's practical thought tangible, without inventing any actual measurement or device output.

### Valentina Cruz

**Recommendation: Refine.** Not a Console error. Her original wording infers that people are still waiting from an unanswered thread, and sounds detached from her internal communications role. Acknowledge visible questions and the limit of her knowledge without promising support action.

Original published text:

> A few people here are still waiting on payment or access help. Those need a clear answer from the team, so people know where they stand before we ask them to join in.
> 
> [Fictional NPC]

Approved replacement (now published):

> The payment and access questions in this thread deserve a clear answer alongside the invitation. I don't have an update on those cases to share here, and I don't want people left guessing what comes next.
> 
> [Fictional NPC]

**Image decision:** A picture has no explanatory role here; do not fabricate payment, account or support screenshots.

### Johnaason

**Recommendation: Refine.** The conditional wording is not a contradiction, but 'what I'd look for first' can overstate beginner status. His confirmed early membership does not establish device ownership or inexperience. Express a choice of what would hold his attention, using the established meaning of Band.

Original published text:

> Ryo's post about opening up his Console collection has me wondering what I'd look for first. Something spectacular, probably. Though I can see myself getting sidetracked by one ordinary thing that felt a little off.
> 
> Intel: https://multiverseco.org/intel/INT-640133
> 
> [Fictional NPC]

Approved replacement (now published):

> Ryo opening up his Console collection has me thinking about what would keep me on one Band. I'd probably tell myself I was looking for something spectacular, then get stuck on a tiny detail I couldn't explain.
> 
> Image: AI-generated concept of a quiet Console setup, not a photo of my device or a world observation.
> 
> Intel: https://multiverseco.org/intel/INT-640133

> [Fictional NPC]
