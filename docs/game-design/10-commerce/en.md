# Current Voyager Pack pricing and entitlements

Confirmed October 21, 2026: **Initial US$38**, **Complete US$488**, and **Console + Pack 4 upgrade US$460 for every Initial member, including historical $12 buyers**. One-time payments; no recurring membership fee. Previously completed $520/$400 purchases retain their actual payments and terms, with no retroactive charge.

| Purchase | New payment | Included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial | $38 | Member digital access and Packs 1–2: welcome letter, badge and Antenna for Multiverse Console; no Console |
| Complete | $488 | The same member access and all four Packs, including one Console claim |
| Initial upgrade | $460 | Console and Pack 4; keep the first two Packs without duplication |

Initial plus upgrade totals $498; Complete saves $10. This is a fixed upgrade price, not a subtraction of the earlier payment. Initial occupies one seat; upgrading reuses it. Console access requires a confirmed Complete or upgrade payment. New orders use pricing version `voyager-bundles-v1`; historical receipts remain verifiable under their saved version.

Delivery is limited to the contiguous 48 US states and DC, with shipping and applicable taxes included. New cancellation refunds allocate each payment equally across the packs it purchases: Initial $19 per pack, Complete $122, upgrade $230. Original Initial payments remain separate from upgrade refunds; historical terms remain applicable.

Local integration does not mean production pricing has changed. Migration `schema_v103.sql` is not applied; dedicated Stripe prices and production Checkout acceptance remain pending. See the new pricing release record.

## Historical record

October 22, 2026 naming clarification: the base name is **Antenna**, introduced as **Antenna for Multiverse Console**. An emblem of the Collective and a decorative collectible, it receives a particular category of parallel-world signals when installed on the Console. It cannot receive signals independently. The Collective's knowledge of the signals is limited; members explore them together. The three illustrated finishes are design studies, not a promise of three items or selectable finishes in Pack 2. Final appearance and quantity remain to be confirmed. See the [product language record](../../product/antenna.zh.md).

The previous $12 / $520 / $400 offer and its dated rollout notes are preserved in the [historical pricing archive](pricing-before-20261021.en.md). They do not price new orders.
