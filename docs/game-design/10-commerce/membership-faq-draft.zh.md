## 英文 FAQ

### 1 Membership payment

**Is this a subscription?**

No. Initiation is a one-time membership payment of $520, with no renewal fees. Future devices, activities and other optional offerings may be sold separately.

### 2 Console entitlement

**Is a Console included?**

Yes. Initiation includes one Console claim, with no additional device payment. Choose an available batch on the Device page. If a batch is full, you can choose another available batch or wait. Your unclaimed entitlement is preserved.

### 3 Console batch changes

**Can I change my Console batch?**

You can request a batch change before your Console ships, subject to availability. Once shipped, your Console is bound to your account and the batch cannot be changed.

### 4 Shipping schedule

**When will my packs ship?**

The four S26 packs are planned to ship in October, November and December 2026, then January 2027. These are planned shipping months, not guaranteed arrival dates. The Console is Pack 3. No S26 packs have shipped yet.

### 5 Shipping region and costs

**Where do you ship?**

We ship to the contiguous 48 US states and Washington, DC. Shipping and applicable taxes are included. Enter your delivery address at checkout.

### 6 Initial Pack members

**I already bought the $12 Initial Pack. What changes?**

You can complete Initiation for $400—a $120 discount. Until then, you keep Intel access, member voting and your first two packs. Completing Initiation adds the Console claim and fourth pack.

### 7 Refunds

**How are refunds calculated?**

For refunds, your Initiation payment is divided equally across the four packs—25% per pack. Your refund is based on the packs that have not yet been fulfilled.

## 退款计算示例

采用实付 Initiation 金额均分的拟定口径：新会员 $520，每包 $130；旧会员本次补款 $400，每包 $100。原 $12 订单不并入本次计算。均分用于退款计算，不代表各包的单独售价。

| 未履约包数 | 退款比例 | 实付 $520 | 实付 $400 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 100% | $520 | $400 |
| 3 | 75% | $390 | $300 |
| 2 | 50% | $260 | $200 |
| 1 | 25% | $130 | $100 |
| 0 | 0% | $0 | $0 |

这张表仅解释按未履约包数取消时的计算方式，不表示损坏、错发、缺件、遗失等售后情形一律不退款；这些情形仍需单独确定。

## 需要审阅的边界

- **履约时点**：退款从哪一刻扣掉该包的 25%——交给承运人，还是确认送达？英文暂用 “fulfilled”，没有替用户选定时点。
- **旧会员补款退款**：表格暂按 $400 实付金额均分。原有前两包即使不补款也应履约；若它们已经寄出，之后补款再取消，是否仍扣除补款中的前两包份额，需要明确。不能因退补款而自动取消原 $12 的独立权益。
- **退款后的权限**：全额或部分退款后，成员身份、Intel、投票、S26 席位和未使用的 Console claim 如何处理，尚需统一。
- **发货前换批次**：拟用 “request” 表示申请更换，受库存限制；是否通过 voyagers@multiverseco.org 受理，以及具体处理流程，尚需确定。目前代码在确认 Claim 后禁止自行更换，不能把草稿视为该流程已经实现。
- **设备绑定时点**：最新意图为发货后绑定设备，绑定后才是 Console Holder。需要核对并统一 Claim 时的库存分配、账号关联与正式设备绑定的区别，再调整产品逻辑。
- **Console 寄送计划**：若十二月仍未 Claim，或所选批次暂无库存，第三包具体何时寄出及如何通知仍需确定；不承诺自动排队或保证十二月寄出。
