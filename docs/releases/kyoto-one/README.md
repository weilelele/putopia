## Media

Assets are in `public/assets/kyoto-one-20260910/`. The hero is generated with the built-in image tool, based on original device geometry and approved BG-01-N-v5 warehouse background. The other three assets are original design references, not unit condition photographs.

The hero was visually checked for the low wedge housing, thin upper rim, orange tab, side controls, normal continuous wood grain, contact shadows and absence of location signage. It is a design visualization; side-screen pixels are not a specification of the delivered software. Older warehouse composites with repetitive crackle textures were excluded.
